About Kandy

A historic city where heritage, nature, culture and urban life come together.

Where History Meets Nature

Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most important historic cities. Surrounded by green hills, the city has a unique relationship between natural landscapes and urban development.

Kandy was the last royal capital of Sri Lanka and remains an important centre of religion, culture, education, tourism and commerce.

The historic city centre, Kandy Lake, Temple of the Tooth and surrounding hills create a distinctive urban landscape.

Places to Visit

Explore the landmarks and landscapes that make Kandy unique.

01

Temple of the Tooth

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Dalada Maligawa) is one of Sri Lanka’s most sacred Buddhist sites, located in the heart of Kandy beside the historic Kandy Lake. It houses the sacred tooth relic of the Buddha and is a major cultural, religious, and historical landmark, attracting pilgrims and visitors from around the world.

02

Kandy Lake

Kandy Lake Round is a scenic road that circles the historic Kandy Lake, connecting several important landmarks and public spaces in the heart of Kandy. It is a popular area for walking, sightseeing, and experiencing Kandy’s historic landscape, with views of the lake, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, and surrounding hills.

03

Queen's Hotel

Queen's Hotel is a historic colonial-style hotel in the heart of Kandy, overlooking Kandy Lake and located opposite the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. With over 160 years of history, it is one of Kandy’s most iconic heritage landmarks, known for its distinctive colonial architecture and cultural significance.

04

Bahirawakanda

Bahirawakanda Temple is a prominent Buddhist temple situated on a hill overlooking Kandy, famous for its large white Buddha statue that can be seen from many parts of the city. The temple offers panoramic views of Kandy and its surrounding hills, making it both an important religious site and a popular scenic attraction.

TRADITION & HERITAGE

Culture & Heritage

Kandy's cultural identity is deeply connected with its history, religion and traditions.

A Living Cultural Landscape

Kandy is strongly associated with Sri Lankan Buddhist traditions, historic institutions, traditional performing arts and cultural celebrations.

The Temple of the Tooth and the Kandy Esala Perahera are particularly important elements of the city's cultural identity.

The relationship between heritage buildings, streets, public spaces and surrounding landscapes creates a strong sense of place.
